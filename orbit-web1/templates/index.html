ABOUT US

A shared read on ore potential, for the departments who act on it.

Manganese exploration usually means stitching together separate geological and weather reports by hand. OreBit gives mining directorates and meteorological officials one shared view of a region's potential — and the data behind it.

Built for officials

Designed around how mining directorates and meteorological departments actually review a site — fast reads, clear numbers, exportable summaries.

Geology + climate, together

Combines soil composition, rock type, and historical mining records with rainfall and terrain moisture instead of treating them as separate reports.

Meteorological context

Surfaces the seasonal and terrain signals that affect both ore accessibility and survey planning — not just a static geology score.

A decision aid, not a black box

Every score comes with the supporting factors behind it, so an official can see why a zone was flagged before acting on it.

HOW IT WORKS

From satellite and terrain data to a decision-ready score

01

Data Ingestion

Sentinel-2 spectral bands (B2 to B12), elevation, and meteorological moisture metrics are gathered for target coordinates.

02

Coordinate Targeting

Pick any location directly on the interactive map or submit exact coordinates across any state in India.

03

AI-Driven Scoring

The trained ensemble model transforms multispectral indices (NDVI, NDWI, Ferric Oxide, SWIR ratios) to predict manganese deposit probability.

04

Decision Output

Potential tier (High/Medium/Low), estimated tonnage range, confidence score, and geological factors ready for export.
